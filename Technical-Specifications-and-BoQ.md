# Technology Procurement Case Study

**Project:** Desktop Computer Procurement  
**Client:** Hudson Enterprise (Hypothetical Case Study)  
**Prepared by:** Haider Ali  
**Role:** Technology Procurement & Commercial Advisory  
**Project Status:** Portfolio Example — In Progress

## 1. Project Overview

Hudson Enterprise requires 150 desktop computers for its business operations. The objective is to document a structured procurement process that supports technical suitability, supplier comparison, budget control, and informed purchasing decisions.

Ali’s Computers acts only as a procurement advisory service provider in this scenario. The client selects and pays the supplier directly.

## 2. Procurement Objectives

- Confirm business and technical requirements.
- Prepare clear technical specifications.
- Request quotations from suitable suppliers.
- Compare supplier offers fairly.
- Assess total cost and delivery terms.
- Present a recommendation for client approval.
- Maintain clear procurement records.

## 4. Procurement Workflow

1. Receive and review the procurement request.
2. Confirm missing requirements with stakeholders.
3. Prepare technical specifications and Bill of Quantities (BoQ).
4. Issue a Request for Quotation (RFQ).
5. Receive and evaluate supplier quotations.
6. Compare technical compliance, price, warranty, and delivery.
7. Prepare a cost comparison and recommendation.
8. Obtain client approval before purchase.
9. Track delivery and document final outcomes.

## 5. Supplier Evaluation Criteria

- Technical compliance
- Total quoted price
- Warranty and after-sales support
- Delivery lead time
- Payment terms
- Supplier reliability and relevant experience

Supplier scores and quotations will be recorded only after actual or clearly labelled sample offers are available.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

**Client:** Confirms requirements, approves the recommendation, selects the supplier, and pays the supplier directly.

**Procurement Advisor:** Coordinates documentation, quotation comparison, commercial analysis, and recommendation.

**Supplier:** Provides accurate quotations, confirms product specifications, and fulfils the agreed order.

## 7. Risks and Controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Incomplete specifications | Obtain written client confirmation |
| Unclear warranty | Request warranty terms in writing |
| Price changes | Confirm quotation validity |
| Delivery delays | Obtain a committed delivery schedule |
| Unapproved purchase | Require client approval before ordering |

## 8. Deliverables

- Project Brief
- Technical Specifications and BoQ
- Request for Quotation (RFQ)
- Supplier Evaluation and Comparison
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis
- Procurement Recommendation
- Procurement Status Report
